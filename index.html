What it does

A short list — the full, up-to-date one lives in the repo's feature checklist.

End-to-end encrypted

Signal Protocol with post-quantum prekeys. The server never has the keys to read anything, even if compelled.

Tor-only transport

Everything routes through a Tor hidden service. No direct device-to-device link, and the server never learns a real IP.

Duress password

Entering it instead of your real password silently wipes the device — no visible sign anything happened.

Sealed media

Burn-after-read photos and video: the recipient taps to reveal, then it's deleted from the server.

One-time QR pairing

Single-use, atomically consumed — a pairing code can't be intercepted and replayed.

Brute-force lockout

4 free wrong guesses, then escalating waits — 30s, 2m, 10m, up to 30m.

Private vault

Save photos and videos into one folder per contact, browse them by date, and lock folders behind a separate vault PIN you choose the wrong-guess rules for.

Photo and video editing

Draw, add text, emoji and shapes to photos, and burn text into video before sending. Sealing is your choice, per item.

Password or biometric unlock

Passwords are stretched with Argon2id. Turn on fingerprint or face unlock any time from Settings; either one opens the app.

Delivered and seen receipts

See when a message has reached the other device and when it has been read, with unread counts on your contact list.
